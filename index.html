01 · The setting

Nature and city are meeting in the same landscape.

Kalmar’s planning documents describe Snurrom as a place where nature, city, outdoor life and everyday life meet. Krafslösaskogen is also described by the municipality as a forest area used for recreation.

This survey adds another layer: not what the landscape is planned to become, but how people already experience it.

05 · The central tension

People value coexistence much more than they trust its recognition in governance.

Coexistence & equal valueItems 1–7
Governance recognitionItems 8–11

The first seven multispecies items describe coexistence, equal value, protection and accessibility. Their mean is high.

The final four ask whether city decision-making and governance recognize species and their rights. Their mean drops sharply below the scale midpoint.

Both subscales are internally consistent in this sample (Cronbach’s α shown in the methodology).

06 · Connecting the variables

Attachment is the strongest statistical companion of well-being.

An exploratory respondent-level regression predicts the well-being composite from nature bonding, visit frequency, forest functionality, coexistence orientation, governance recognition, distance and age. Predictors and outcome are standardized; robust HC3 standard errors are used.

Exploratory model
R²

About four-fifths of the variation in the well-being composite is associated with the included predictors in this sample.

This is cross-sectional survey data. Coefficients describe association, not causal effects. Likert composites are treated as approximately continuous.

09 · Method & transparency

What is included — and what is not.

Respondent unit

Descriptive statistics and regression use one row per respondent. Repeated map pins are never counted as separate people.

Quality control

Two submissions with explicit “test” map text are excluded from analysis. Four substantive pins with impossible coordinates are excluded from the map, but their respondent surveys remain in respondent-level analysis. Survey points are aligned to the municipal overview for descriptive storytelling, not cadastral or engineering use.

Scale construction

Item means form composite scores. Internal consistency is strong: bonding α , functionality α , well-being α , coexistence α , governance α .

Interpretation

The sample is self-selected, the design is cross-sectional, and many responses come from frequent local users. Results describe this sample and should not be generalized without a sampling frame.

Technical model details

The model is OLS with heteroscedasticity-consistent HC3 standard errors. Outcome: standardized mean of routine_1–routine_9. Predictors: standardized nature-bonding mean, harmonized ordinal visit frequency, functionality mean, multispecies coexistence mean (multi_1–multi_7), governance-recognition mean (multi_8–multi_11), residential-distance ordinal code, and age-band ordinal code.
